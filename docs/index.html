Leave home calmer.

Stop wondering "did I lock the door?" CalmMaker is a small, private app that helps you check what matters before leaving — and look back at proof when anxiety strikes.

What it does

Built for the moment you stop at the door.

Tiny rituals to ease the "did I leave the iron on?" loop. Everything stays on your phone.

✅

Smart checklists

Pre-built lists for security, appliances, things to take, pets — or build your own from scratch.

📸

Photo & video proof

Snap the locked door, the iron unplugged, the closed window. Look back any time you need reassurance.

🧹

Auto-cleanup

Entries vanish on a schedule you set — 1 day, 7 days, custom. No clutter, no junk in your gallery.

🔒

Local-only privacy

Your photos and recordings never leave your device. No cloud, no upload, no account required.

🌍

Eight languages

English, Polski, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, 日本語, 中文.

🌗

Light & dark themes

Easy on the eyes, day or night. Follows your system theme automatically.

How it works

Three taps before the door closes.

No accounts, no setup screens. Open the app, check the list, walk out.

Pick a checklist

Use a ready template or one you've built — doors, stove, windows, pet bowls, whatever matters to you.

Check & capture

Tick items off. Add a photo or short video for the ones you want proof of later.

Look back later

If doubt creeps in mid-day, open the entry and see for yourself. The entry deletes itself when its timer ends.

Your data stays on your phone.

CalmMaker doesn't have user accounts, doesn't ask for sign-in, and doesn't upload your photos or recordings anywhere. Read the full privacy policy.
