How it works

Five steps to a calmer departure.

No accounts, no sign-in. Open the app, check the list, walk out.

Pick an entry

A quick entry or a ready checklist — whatever fits the moment.

Check & capture

Tick items off, add photos, or record a short video of what you want to remember.

Set the timer

Choose how long the entry stays visible — 1 day, the length of your trip, custom.

Look back if needed

If doubt creeps in mid-day, open the entry and see for yourself.

Forget it

After your set time, the entry and its photos vanish automatically.

Who it's for

For anyone who sometimes goes back to check just one more time.

Pre-departure stress is a common experience — also for people with OCD, ADHD, or on the autism spectrum. CalmMaker helps you organize the routines that already work for you.

Important. CalmMaker is a supportive, organizational tool. It does not replace medical advice, psychotherapy, or treatment. If you struggle with OCD, ADHD, or significant anxiety, please consider reaching out to a qualified professional.

Your data stays on your phone.

No accounts, no sign-in, your photos and recordings are never uploaded anywhere. Read the full privacy policy.
